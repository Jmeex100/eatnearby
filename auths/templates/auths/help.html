How to Join or Contact Us

Interested in becoming a part of Eat-NEAR-BY?
Here's how you can get started

  1. 1
    Contact us through any of the channels listed above—phone, WhatsApp, email, or visit us in person.
  2. 2
    Speak with one of our staff members to express your interest in joining or learning more about our services.
  3. 3
    Provide your contact information for registration purposes.
  4. 4
    Wait for a confirmation message, which will be sent to you via email or phone.

We're excited to welcome you to our community and look forward to serving you!

How to Order

Follow these simple steps to order our products:

  1. 1

    Go to the menu and add items to your order.

  2. 2

    Check your cart to see your order and total.

  3. 3

    Select your payment method.

  4. 4

    Complete payment to finalize your order.

Thank you for your order! We’re excited to serve you!
